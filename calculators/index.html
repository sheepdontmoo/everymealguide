Why compare the delivered cost?

Meal services package prices differently. Some lead with a per-serving figure, some promote a heavily discounted first box, and some charge flat delivery. A consistent calculation exposes the recurring amount and makes differently sized boxes comparable.

These tools do not decide whether a service suits your diet or routine. Use the result alongside our meal delivery rankings, reviews and cancellation guidance.
